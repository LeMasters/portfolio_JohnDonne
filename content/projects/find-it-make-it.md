On a summer workshop in London, four of us built a small browser game for a modern-art museum's family visitors: find shapes and colours from three paintings in the room, then make your own composition. I designed the screens in Figma, painted the backgrounds and ran two of the four test sessions. We tested with 18 visitors over two Saturdays, and the museum's education officer asked to keep the prototype for a school-holiday event.
