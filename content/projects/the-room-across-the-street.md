At the frame shop I sketched a window full of frames for our summer display. My boss stood outside and asked which one we actually sold. In the second drawing I painted one oversize gilt frame and left its center clear. From the sidewalk you could see a real corner sample hanging inside it. I lettered the hours below the sill line and transferred the drawing from a paper pattern. The shop owner chose and hung the samples
