A four-person IMS213 team game: talk to the regulars while the machines run. I was the sole artist: six painted backgrounds, the HUD and menus (14 Figma screens, built in Unreal's UMG), and three character turnarounds with expression sheets. In week 9 I rebuilt the UI colours from the background palette so the menus stopped looking pasted on.
